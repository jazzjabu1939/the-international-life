WHOLE CITY IN THERE

KYOTO

A course plan for learning from a city rather than merely passing through it.

35.0116° N / 135.7681° E
JAPAN / FALL 2026

01 / CITY QUESTION

What becomes visible when attention is treated as a discipline?

Kyoto is not offered here as timeless Japan or as scenery for self-discovery. We will examine a living city shaped by ritual, tourism, universities, commerce, preservation, ordinary work, and contested ideas about tradition.

02 / LEARNING ROUTE

PREPARE

Arrive with better questions

Locate Kyoto historically and geographically; learn essential courtesies and the difference between a cultural claim and a cultural fact.

ENTER

Find the ordinary city

Notice transit, convenience stores, neighborhoods, university life, work rhythms, and the systems behind the famous image.

NOTICE

Practice slow attention

Keep a field record of sounds, signs, objects, rules, gestures, misunderstandings, and changes in your own perception.

INTERPRET

Test the first impression

Compare what you saw with local voices, historical sources, and evidence that complicates the packaged story.

RECIPROCATE

Ask what is owed

Treat residents as people rather than cultural material. Credit the human doors through which understanding became possible.

RETURN

Carry something changed

Translate experience into conduct, relationship, and a next action—not merely a photograph or polished anecdote.

03 / KYOTO COURSE PLAN

Four encounters with the city

  1. 01

    The city we think we know

    Gibson’s virtual Kyoto, tourism’s visual shorthand, and the danger of mistaking recognition for knowledge.

  2. 02

    Old Kyoto / living infrastructure

    Temples, ceremony, preservation, neighborhood life, and tradition as a changing social system.

  3. 03

    The discipline of attention

    A Temple Pause and field-note exercise: looking slowly enough for assumptions to become visible.

  4. 04

    The human door

    One person’s placed experience, ethically represented; preparation for the student’s Sixth Tape.
