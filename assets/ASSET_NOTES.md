# Visual asset notes

The four production backgrounds were generated August 14, 2026 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool, then converted to WebP at quality 84 for the browser experience. They contain no generated text or logos.

## `darwin-dusk.webp`

Prompt summary: late-1980s Darwin at humid amber dusk; tropical coastline, distant airport approach lights and tiny aircraft; editorial screen-print/analog-film treatment; quiet dark left half for interface typography; no people, signage, logos, or modern architecture.

## `virtual-kyoto.webp`

Prompt summary: a dreamlike late-1980s cassette-memory of Kyoto; raked gravel and fifteen stones, traditional roof silhouettes, vermilion gate, waterfall mist; blue-green editorial screen-print/analog-video treatment; quiet dark left half for interface typography; no people, signage, logos, anime, or neon cyberpunk treatment.

## `anonymous-mall.webp`

Prompt summary: a late-1980s free-trade-zone concourse whose generic licensed shops could be almost anywhere; one irregular folding table and a distant security guard; tobacco and institutional-brass screen-print treatment; quiet dark left half for interface typography; no readable brands, crowds, or modern screens.

## `cassette-vendor.webp`

Prompt summary: a dignified, sun-scarred elderly vendor at a battered card table, weathered hands among scratched cassette cases, with a small distant guard; warm brass and oxidized-green screen-print treatment; quiet dark left half for interaction controls; no violence, readable labels, logos, or futuristic technology.

These are original illustrative composites, not documentary depictions of a single real view. The interface's Sources & Credits dialog separately identifies the boundary between its imagined prologue and the material adapted from William Gibson.
# Darwin main arrivals hall

`darwin-arrivals-hall.webp` was generated August 14, 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool for the public-arrivals interaction. It depicts a visually dense late-1980s Darwin terminal with the information desk discoverable in the middle-right distance. The production prompt required period clothing, analog signage and technology, an approachable attendant, humid dusk, and no modern screens, contemporary brands, weapons, or watermarks. The selected PNG was converted locally to WebP at quality 82 (about 188 KB).
