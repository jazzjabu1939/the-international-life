# The International Life — Darwin Opening and Tape Branch

## Product identity

The experience is **The International Life**. Darwin is the opening location and teaches the student how the interface works. Virtual Kyoto and Virtual Taipei are cassette-borne city worlds inside the larger experience.

## Canonical student sequence

1. **Title — The International Life**
   - Establish late-1980s Darwin, the student's limited cash, and the destination: the brother's apartment.
   - Primary action: begin arrival.

2. **Darwin airport — baggage carousel**
   - Student scans the restored panoramic baggage artwork.
   - Several bags can be inspected; the khaki backpack with the faded UMass patch is the correct one.
   - Collecting it updates inventory and unlocks the arrivals hall.

3. **Darwin airport — main arrivals**
   - Student confronts visually competing signs and must locate the information desk.
   - A bounded attendant answers practical questions and presents transport choices.
   - Taxi is the canonical story route to the brother's apartment; bus and shuttle can remain meaningful budget alternatives if the subsequent writing accounts for them.

4. **Journey through Darwin**
   - Fare is deducted from the wallet.
   - A short atmospheric transition shows unfamiliar streets, heat, light, and partial orientation.

5. **Brother's apartment**
   - Student arrives, encounters the anonymous console, glasses, and gloves, but has no cassette yet.
   - The brother or room provides the reason to walk to the mall.
   - Objective becomes: explore the Darwin Free Trade Zone.

6. **Walk to the mall**
   - A short sequence conveys distance, heat, brands, and the transition from street to licensed commercial space.
   - This is not free-roaming 3D navigation; it is a sequence of strong images and clear actions.

7. **Darwin Free Trade Zone mall**
   - Student scans the polished mall and finds the unlicensed old man at its edge.
   - The contrast between licensed global brands and handmade tapes is explicit.

8. **Old man's cassette table**
   - “Whole city in there” is the refrain.
   - At least two real purchases are available: **Virtual Kyoto** and **Virtual Taipei**.
   - Each tape has a handmade physical identity and costs money from the same wallet.
   - The choice is consequential, not a right-answer test.

9. **Return to the brother's apartment**
   - Student slots the selected cassette into the anonymous console and puts on glasses and gloves.
   - The refrain returns as the room gives way to the selected city.

10. **City threshold**
    - Kyoto tape opens Virtual Kyoto.
    - Taipei tape opens Virtual Taipei.
    - Each threshold leads into a city-specific course plan built from the shared course template.

11. **City course world**
    - For this phase, each branch must at minimum have a coherent landing page, city question, encounters, resources, and a clear next action.
    - Later automation may add saved progress, field notes, bounded guides, and the Sixth Tape synthesis. Those features must not be represented as complete until they work in the interface.

## Phase-one completion test

A first-time student can start at **The International Life**, finish the Darwin airport and mall sequence without verbal rescue, buy either Kyoto or Taipei, enter the matching city, and understand what the course asks them to do next.
