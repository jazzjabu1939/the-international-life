01 / CITY QUESTION

How do we begin well when fluency, certainty, and permission have not yet arrived?

Taipei is not offered as an easy gateway to “Asia” or as a nostalgic backdrop. We will encounter a living city shaped by migration, language, markets, temples, democratic argument, Japanese and Chinese histories, global technology, ordinary work, and competing accounts of identity.

02 / LEARNING ROUTE

PREPARE

Learn the unfinished map

Locate Taipei historically and geographically; recognize that names, borders, languages, and political claims carry different meanings for different people.

ENTER

Use what language you have

Practice asking, listening, getting lost, repairing mistakes, and noticing what changes when a relationship begins in another language.

NOTICE

Read the street in layers

Keep field notes on scooters, signs, shopfronts, transit, food, weather, temples, work rhythms, and the vertical city above eye level.

INTERPRET

Complicate the first story

Compare personal memory and visual shorthand with local voices, historical evidence, and disagreements that resist a single tidy explanation.

RECIPROCATE

Remember the human door

Treat hosts, teachers, classmates, vendors, and strangers as people rather than access points. Credit what they made possible.

RETURN

Keep the relationship alive

Translate experience into sustained language study, correspondence, conduct, and a next action that outlasts the travel story.

03 / TAIPEI COURSE PLAN

Four encounters with the city

  1. 01

    Taipei before fluency

    Arrival, partial language, productive embarrassment, and the practical courage of beginning before competence feels complete.

  2. 02

    Many histories on one street

    Indigenous Taiwan, Qing and Japanese rule, the Republic of China, migration after 1949, democratization, and the stories held in buildings and names.

  3. 03

    The ordinary global city

    Night markets, schools, temples, factories, semiconductor networks, convenience systems, and the labor beneath frictionless modernity.

  4. 04

    The person who opened the door

    One relationship told with consent, specificity, and humility; preparation for the student’s Sixth Tape.

05 / MAKE THE NEXT TAPE

Beginning is not the opposite of knowing. It is how knowing starts.

The final tape does not exist yet. Find its person, enter its place, and make it with enough specificity and care that someone else can hear a whole city inside it.
