SOURCE BOUNDARY

What is imagined, and what is Gibson?

The airport, journey, brother's apartment, and walk to the mall form an imagined prologue. They are not presented as passages from William Gibson.

The mall, unlicensed vendor, scratched cassette, and virtual-city threshold adapt the Kelsey episode in William Gibson's Academy Leader (1991), published in Cyberspace: First Steps. Virtual Taipei is an original course branch, not a destination named in the story.

This classroom prototype uses original code and abstract environment art. It quotes no extended passage from the story.
